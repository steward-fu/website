微處理器 - Altera 10M02SCM (STEP-MAX10 V1) - 解決USB-Blaster無法偵測到的問題(Linux)


參考資訊：
https://rocketboards.org/foswiki/Documentation/UsingUSBBlasterUnderLinux
https://unix.stackexchange.com/questions/39370/how-to-reload-udev-rules-without-reboot

USB-Blaster在Debian下出現Unable to scan device chain. Can't scan JTAG chain時，可以透過添加udev rule解決，步驟如下： 

$ sudo vim /etc/udev/rules.d/92-usbblaster.rules
    SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="09fb", ATTRS{idProduct}=="6001", MODE="0666"
    SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="09fb", ATTRS{idProduct}=="6002", MODE="0666"
    SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="09fb", ATTRS{idProduct}=="6003", MODE="0666"
    SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="09fb", ATTRS{idProduct}=="6010", MODE="0666"
    SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="09fb", ATTRS{idProduct}=="6810", MODE="0666"

$ sudo udevadm control --reload-rules
$ sudo udevadm trigger
