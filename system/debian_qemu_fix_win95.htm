系統 - Debian - QEMU - 解決Windows 95 Windows Protection Error問題


參考資訊：
https://github.com/utmapp/UTM/discussions/5191
https://archive.org/download/fix-95-cpu-v3-final

解法如下： 

$ cd
$ wget https://archive.org/download/fix-95-cpu-v3-final/FIX95CPU_V3_FINAL.ZIP
$ qemu-system-i386 -hda FIX95CPU.IMA ...

A:\> FIX95CPU.BAT
