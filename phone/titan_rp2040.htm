手機 - Unihertz Titan (TEE-EMMC) - 如何掛載Raspberry Pi RP2 Boot(RP2040)


$ sudo blkid
    /dev/block/sda1: SEC_TYPE="msdos" LABEL_FATBOOT="RPI-RP2" LABEL="RPI-RP2" UUID="0009-7F6A" BLOCK_SIZE="512" TYPE="vfat" PARTUUID="00097f6a-01"

$ sudo mount /dev/block/sda1 /mnt/
$ ls /mnt/
    INDEX.HTM  INFO_UF2.TXT
