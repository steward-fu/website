手機 - Astro Slide 5G - Android - 解決Your device will boot in 5 seconds問題


參考資訊：
https://github.com/planet-community-org/planet-devices/wiki/Astro-rooting
https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/How+to+unlock+the+bootloader+of+an+Android+Phone/152629

問題如下： 

Your device has been unlocked and cannot be trusted
Your device will boot in 5 seconds

解法如下：
執行如下命令後，再將Astro Slide 5G連接到PC

$ cd
$ git clone https://github.com/bkerler/mtkclient
$ cd mtkclient
$ ./mtk r vbmeta_a vbmeta_a_astro-slide-5g.img
$ ./mtk w vbmeta_a vbmeta.img.empty
